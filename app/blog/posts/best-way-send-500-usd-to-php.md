## The mid-market rate (your baseline)

The mid-market rate is the real exchange rate — the one you see on Google. As of August 2026, 1 USD = approximately ₱61.58 PHP. This is your benchmark. Any provider giving you less than this rate is pocketing the difference.

Most providers advertise "no fees" or "low fees" but make their money by giving you a worse exchange rate. We'll show you both: the fee they charge AND the rate they give.

### 1. Wise — Best overall for cost transparency

[**Send with Wise →**](https://wise.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Wise is the only provider that gives you the true mid-market rate. Their fee is upfront — $4.15 on a $500 transfer (0.83%) — and there's no hidden markup in the exchange rate. What you see is what you get.

- **Best for:** Senders who want the most pesos to arrive and don't need instant delivery

- **The catch:** Not instant. If your family needs money right now, Wise won't be fast enough.
- **Pro tip:** Wise's fee percentage drops as amounts increase. At $1,000, it's still 0.83%. At $5,000+, it falls to ~0.45%.

### 2. Remitly Express — Best for speed

[**Send with Remitly →**](https://remitly.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Remitly's Express service delivers in minutes — straight to GCash, Maya, bank, or cash pickup. The fee is $3.99, but the exchange rate carries a small margin (~0.6%). On $500, you pay slightly more than Wise but get delivery in minutes.

- **Best for:** Emergency transfers, GCash delivery, first-time senders
- **Delivery:** Instant (minutes) to GCash, bank, or cash pickup
- **The catch:** The rate isn't the mid-market. You lose roughly ₱100–₱200 to the FX margin.
- **Pro tip:** Remitly frequently runs first-transfer promos — zero fees on your first send. Check the homepage before signing up.

### 3. Remitly Economy — Best "no fee" option

[**Send with Remitly →**](https://remitly.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Remitly's Economy tier charges $0 in fees but uses a wider exchange rate margin (~1.3%). It's cheaper than Express in total cost, but takes 3–5 business days. The "free" transfer isn't free — you pay via the rate.

- **Best for:** Non-urgent transfers when you want zero upfront fees
- **Delivery:** 3–5 business days to bank or GCash
- **The catch:** You lose more to the FX margin than you save on the fee. On $500, the margin costs you ~₱390 vs. Wise's ₱0 margin.

### 4. WorldRemit — Best for cash pickup

[**Send with WorldRemit →**](https://worldremit.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

WorldRemit charges $3.99 and has a ~0.8% FX margin. It's not the cheapest, but it excels at one thing: cash pickup. With 15,000+ pickup locations across the Philippines (Palawan, Cebuana, M Lhuillier), it's ideal when your recipient doesn't have a bank account or GCash.

- **Best for:** Cash pickup, recipients without bank accounts
- **Delivery:** Instant to 4 hours for cash pickup; instant for GCash
- **The catch:** Higher total cost than Wise and Remitly Economy. Also supports airtime top-up (unique feature).
- **Pro tip:** WorldRemit often has referral promos — both you and your invitee get a discount.

### 5. Western Union (online) — Best known, not best value

[**Send with Western Union →**](https://westernunion.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Western Union has the largest agent network in the Philippines — over 10,000 locations. Their online service charges $0 fees (bank-funded) but applies a ~1.8% exchange rate margin. That's the highest markup in this comparison.

- **Best for:** Recipients who only use cash pickup and are near a WU location
- **Delivery:** Instant to 10 minutes for cash pickup; instant for GCash
- **The catch:** The "zero fee" is misleading. The 1.8% margin on $500 costs you ~₱540 — more than any explicit fee in this list.
- **Pro tip:** Never use Western Union's cash counter (in-person send). The fee jumps to $15+ and the margin widens to ~3%.

### 6. Xoom (PayPal) — Convenient if you already use PayPal

[**Send with Xoom →**](https://xoom.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Xoom is PayPal's remittance service. It charges $4.99 and applies a ~1.1% margin. It's not the cheapest, but if you already have a PayPal balance, it's one click away. Supports bank deposit, GCash, cash pickup, and even home delivery in some areas.

- **Best for:** Existing PayPal users who want convenience
- **Delivery:** Instant to 1 day
- **The catch:** Most expensive option here after Western Union cash. If cost matters, use Wise or Remitly instead.

## How to save even more

1. **Check rates every time.** The cheapest provider changes. What was best last month might not be best today.
2. **Use bank transfer, not card.** Paying by debit/credit card adds 2–3% in surcharges on most providers.
3. **Watch for promos.** Remitly and WorldRemit run first-transfer-free promotions regularly.
4. **Send larger amounts less often.** Fixed fees hurt more on small transfers. A $1,000 transfer once costs less than two $500 transfers.
5. **Set up a rate alert.** USD/PHP moves 1–2% weekly. [Get notified](/rate-alerts) when the rate is in your favor.
