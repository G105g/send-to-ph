## What "instant" actually means

When a provider says "instant," they mean the recipient gets a notification and access to the money within minutes. In practice:

- **GCash/Maya delivery:** Money lands in the wallet within 1–5 minutes. Your recipient gets an SMS from GCash.
- **Cash pickup:** The reference number is generated instantly. Your recipient can go to the pickup location and collect cash within minutes.
- **Bank deposit:** "Instant" bank deposits depend on the receiving bank's processing. BPI, BDO, and Metrobank usually process within minutes. Smaller rural banks can take 1–2 hours.

## What slows down a transfer

### 1. How you pay

The payment method is the biggest speed factor:

| Payment Method | Speed Impact |
|----------------|-------------|
| **Debit/credit card** | Fastest — funds clear instantly |
| **Bank account (ACH)** | Adds 1–3 business days |
| **Bank wire** | Same day, but expensive |
| **PayPal balance** | Instant (for Xoom) |

If you pay by bank account (ACH), the provider has to wait for your bank to release the funds — and US ACH takes 1–3 business days. That's why Remitly Economy (which only accepts bank payment) takes 3–5 days, while Remitly Express (which accepts cards) is instant.

### 2. Delivery method

- **GCash/Maya:** Fastest. The money goes directly into the wallet.
- **Cash pickup:** Fast. The reference number is generated immediately; the recipient just walks to a location.
- **Bank deposit:** Varies by bank. Major banks (BPI, BDO, Metrobank) process within hours. Rural banks can take 1–3 days.
- **Home delivery:** Slowest — 1–2 days. Only Xoom offers this, and only in Metro Manila.

### 3. Security checks

First-time senders may face identity verification delays:
- Remitly, Wise, and WorldRemit all require KYC (Know Your Customer) verification on the first transfer
- This can add 1–24 hours to the first transfer
- Subsequent transfers go through without this delay

### 4. Weekends and holidays

Bank transfers don't process on weekends or Philippine holidays. If you send on Friday evening via bank transfer, the money likely won't move until Monday.

GCash and cash pickup are less affected — they process 24/7 in most cases.

## How to get the fastest transfer

1. **Pay by debit card, not bank account.** Card payments clear instantly; ACH takes days.
2. **Send to GCash, not a bank.** Wallet delivery is instant; bank deposits depend on the receiving bank.
3. **Use Remitly Express or WorldRemit.** Both deliver to GCash in minutes with card payment.
4. **Complete verification before you need to send.** First transfers are slower due to KYC checks.
5. **Avoid weekends for bank transfers.** GCash and cash pickup work on weekends; bank deposits may not.
