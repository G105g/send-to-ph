Xoom and Remitly are both popular choices for sending money to the Philippines. Both offer instant delivery, both support GCash and bank deposits, and both advertise "low fees." But they work differently — and one is consistently cheaper than the other.

We compared them on the USD → PHP corridor using real rates as of August 2026.

## The short answer

**Remitly is cheaper than Xoom on almost every transfer.** Xoom charges higher fees and applies a wider exchange rate margin. The only scenario where Xoom wins is if you already have a PayPal balance and value convenience over cost.

## How Xoom works

[**Send with Xoom →**](/go/xoom)

Xoom is PayPal's remittance service. It integrates directly with PayPal, so if you have a PayPal balance or linked bank account, you can send without creating a new account.

**Strengths:**
- No separate account needed if you have PayPal
- Instant delivery to GCash, bank, and cash pickup
- Home delivery in Metro Manila (unique feature — Xoom couriers cash to your recipient's door)
- PayPal purchase protection applies to transfers

**Weaknesses:**
- $4.99 fee on $500 — highest among the major providers
- 1.1% FX margin — worse than Remitly Express (0.6%)
- PayPal account required — you can't use Xoom without one
- PayPal's interface shows the fee but not the exchange rate margin clearly

## How Remitly works

[**Send with Remitly →**](/go/remitly)

Remitly is a purpose-built remittance app. It's designed for one thing: international money transfers. No wallet, no checkout integration — just send money.

**Strengths:**
- Two tiers: Express (instant) and Economy (cheaper, slower)
- Direct GCash and Maya integration — instant delivery to wallets
- First-transfer promos (frequently $0 fee for new users)
- Transparent fee display
- Cash pickup at 15,000+ locations (Express tier)

**Weaknesses:**
- Exchange rate not published — you only see it in the app before sending
- Requires a separate account (not integrated with PayPal)
- Economy tier takes 3–5 days

## When Xoom is the right choice

### You already have a PayPal balance
If you have money sitting in PayPal from eBay sales, freelance work, or a friend who paid you back, Xoom lets you send it to the Philippines without transferring to your bank first. That convenience is worth the extra cost if it saves you a PayPal withdrawal fee ($0 for >₱7,000, but fees apply for smaller amounts).

### You need home delivery
Xoom is the only provider that offers cash delivery to your recipient's door in Metro Manila. If your recipient can't leave the house (elderly, sick, or unsafe area), this is genuinely useful. Remitly doesn't offer this.

### You want PayPal purchase protection
Xoom transfers are covered by PayPal's protection policies. For some senders — especially first-timers — this adds peace of mind. In practice, Remitly is equally safe, but the PayPal brand trust is real.

## When Remitly is the right choice

### Every other scenario

- **Cheaper:** Lower fees, tighter FX margin
- **Faster (Economy option):** $0 fee tier that Xoom doesn't offer
- **Better GCash integration:** Remitly built its PH service around mobile wallets
- **Cash pickup:** More locations through partner network
- **First-transfer promos:** Frequently offers $0 fee on your first send
