## Step-by-step: Send to BPI with Wise

[**Send with Wise →**](/go/wise)

Best for: Getting the most pesos into the BPI account.

1. **Create a Wise account** at [wise.com](/go/wise). Verify your identity with a US driver's license or passport.

2. **Start a transfer:** Enter USD amount, select PHP as destination currency.

3. **Add recipient:** Select "Bank deposit" → "Philippines" → "BPI." Enter your recipient's full name and 10-digit BPI account number.

4. **Review the rate:** Wise shows the mid-market rate and the fee upfront. On $500, the fee is $4.15 and the rate is exactly ₱61.58.

5. **Pay:** Select bank transfer (cheapest, 1–2 days) or debit card (faster, slightly higher fee).

6. **Wise sends the transfer.** You'll get email/SMS updates. The money arrives in the BPI account within 1–2 business days.

## Step-by-step: Send to BPI with Remitly

2. **Start a transfer:** Enter USD amount, choose Philippines as destination.

3. **Select delivery method:** Choose "Bank Deposit" → search for "BPI."

6. **Pay and send.** Your recipient gets an SMS when the money lands in BPI.

## BPI-specific things to know

### BPI account number format
BPI account numbers are 10 digits. The first digits indicate the account type:
- **001, 002, 003** — Savings account
- **100, 101** — Checking account
- **029** — BPI Family Savings (different from main BPI)

If you're not sure whether your recipient has BPI or BPI Family Savings, ask. They use different bank codes and the transfer could be delayed if you pick the wrong one.

### BPI cut-off times
BPI processes incoming transfers during banking hours. Transfers initiated after 3:00 PM Philippine time (PHT) may be credited the next business day. This mainly affects Wise transfers that arrive in the evening PHT.

### BPI to GCash
If your recipient has both a BPI account and GCash, they can transfer between them instantly using the GCash app (Bank Transfer → BPI → InstaPay). The fee is ₱35 per transfer. This is useful if you send to BPI but they prefer to spend from GCash.

### InstaPay and PESONet
Most providers deliver to BPI via InstaPay (instant, ₱35 fee paid by the provider) or PESONet (batch processing, same-day or next-day). Wise uses InstaPay. Remitly may use either depending on the transfer.
