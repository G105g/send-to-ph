## The fundamental difference

**Wise** gives you the real mid-market exchange rate and charges a separate, transparent fee. You see exactly what you're paying.

**Remitly** bundles the cost into the exchange rate. They may charge $0 in fees, but the rate you get is worse than the mid-market rate. The difference is their margin — and it's not published.

This is the core distinction: Wise shows you the price. Remitly hides it in the rate.

### The pattern

Here's the rule that emerges from the numbers:

- **Small amounts ($100–$300):** Remitly Economy is competitive. The FX margin on small amounts is small in absolute pesos, and $0 fee is attractive.
- **Medium amounts ($500–$1,000):** Wise wins. The transparent fee is less than Remitly's hidden FX margin.
- **Large amounts ($2,000+):** Wise dominates. As Wise's percentage drops, Remitly's margin grows proportionally. On $5,000, you lose ₱3,000+ to Remitly's rate margin.

## The transparency problem

Remitly's biggest weakness isn't price — it's that you can't see the price. Their pricing page shows transfer fees clearly but does not publish the standard exchange rate. You only see the rate they'll give you when you start a transfer in their app.

Wise publishes both the fee and the exchange rate on a public page. You can check the cost before creating an account.

This matters because Remitly's rate changes based on:
- The amount you send
- The delivery method you choose
- Whether it's your first transfer (promotional rate)
- Market conditions

Without a published rate, you can't comparison-shop. You're trusting Remitly to give you a fair price — and the data shows their margin ranges from 0.6% to 1.3% depending on the tier.

## First-transfer promos: Worth it?

Both providers run first-time sender promotions:

- **Remitly:** Frequently offers $0 fee on first transfer (sometimes even a promotional exchange rate for the first $1,000)
- **Wise:** Occasionally offers discounted fees for new users via referral links

If you've never used either, sign up through a referral link and take the promo. But don't let the first-transfer discount lock you in long-term. The promo rate disappears after one transfer, and then you're on the standard pricing — which is where the numbers above apply.

**Our recommendation:** Use the promo for your first transfer. Then compare rates on every subsequent send.
