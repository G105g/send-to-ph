GCash is the wallet of the Philippines. Over 90 million Filipinos use it for groceries, bills, and receiving padala from abroad. If your family has GCash, sending money directly to their wallet is the fastest way to get cash into their hands — no bank account required.

Here's exactly how to do it from the US, which providers work best, and what it costs.

## What you need before you start

- **Your recipient's GCash-registered mobile number** (the one linked to their GCash account)
- **Your recipient's full name** (must match their GCash account)
- **A US bank account or debit card** to fund the transfer
- **Your recipient must have a verified GCash account** (they need to have completed GCash KYC/verification)

If your recipient hasn't verified their GCash account yet, tell them to do it first. Unverified accounts can only receive ₱5,000 per month. Fully verified accounts can receive up to ₱100,000 per month (or more with GSave/GInvest tiers).

Remitly is the easiest way to send to GCash — direct delivery, instant speed, and the widest support for Philippine mobile wallets.

1. **Create a Remitly account** at [remitly.com](https://remitly.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID). You'll need your name, email, and US phone number. Remitly will verify your identity (standard KYC — driver's license or passport).

2. **Start a new transfer.** Enter the amount in USD you want to send.

3. **Select delivery method:** Choose "Mobile Wallet" → "GCash."

4. **Enter your recipient's details:**
   - Full name (must match their GCash account)
   - GCash mobile number (Philippines format: 09XXXXXXXXX)
   - Relationship to you

5. **Choose your service level:**
   - **Express** — $3.99 fee, instant delivery, slightly worse exchange rate
   - **Economy** — $0 fee, 3–5 day delivery, worse exchange rate

6. **Pay for the transfer.** Enter your US bank account or debit card details. Bank transfers are free; card payments may add a surcharge.

7. **Track the transfer.** Remitly sends SMS updates to both you and your recipient. Your recipient gets a text when the money lands in their GCash wallet.

**Total time:** 5 minutes to set up + instant delivery (Express) or 3–5 days (Economy).

## Step-by-step: Sending to GCash with Wise

[**Sign up for Wise →**](https://wise.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID)

Wise doesn't send directly to GCash. Instead, it sends to a Philippine bank via InstaPay, and the recipient forwards to GCash. It's cheaper for large amounts but requires an extra step.

1. **Create a Wise account** at [wise.com](https://wise.com/invite?ref=AFFILIATE_ID).

2. **Set up a transfer:** Enter USD amount, select PHP as the destination currency.

3. **Enter your recipient's bank details:** Philippine bank account (BPI, BDO, Metrobank, etc.) that's linked to InstaPay. Most PH bank accounts are InstaPay-enabled.

4. **Pay** via US bank transfer (cheapest) or debit card.

5. **Wise converts and sends** — 1–2 business days.

6. **Your recipient transfers to GCash:** They open their banking app, use InstaPay to send the pesos to their GCash number. InstaPay fee: ₱35 per transfer.

This method gets the best exchange rate (true mid-market), but the extra step means it's not instant and adds ₱35 to the cost.

**Best for:** Large transfers ($1,000+) where the rate savings outweigh the inconvenience.

## GCash limits to know

Your recipient's GCash account has receiving limits based on verification level:

| Verification Level | Monthly Incoming Limit | Per Transaction |
|--------------------|-----------------------|-----------------|
| **Basic** (unverified) | ₱5,000 | ₱5,000 |
| **Fully Verified** | ₱100,000 | ₱100,000 |
| **GSave/GInvest** | ₱500,000 | ₱500,000 |

If you're sending more than ₱100,000 in a single transfer, your recipient needs a GSave or GInvest account. Most recipients will be at the Fully Verified level — make sure they've completed KYC before you send.

## Common issues and fixes

**"Transfer failed: recipient not verified"**
Your recipient needs to complete GCash verification. They should open the GCash app → Profile → Verify Now. They'll need a valid Philippine ID.

**"Money not received but transfer shows complete"**
GCash occasionally has delays during maintenance windows (usually 11 PM–1 AM Philippine time). Wait 30 minutes and check again. If it doesn't arrive, the recipient should contact GCash support (2882 hotline or in-app chat).

**"Wrong number entered"**
If you sent to the wrong GCash number, contact the provider's support immediately. Remitly and WorldRemit can sometimes cancel a transfer before it's claimed. If the money is already in the wrong account, recovery depends on the recipient returning it — GCash support can help but isn't obligated to reverse it.

**"InstaPay transfer failed (Wise route)"**
InstaPay has a per-transaction limit of ₱50,000. If your Wise transfer is larger than ₱50,000, your recipient will need to do multiple InstaPay transfers to move it all to GCash.
